Speed & flow

Sentence by sentence in hands-free

While you keep talking, each sentence is typed as soon as you pause for about a second.

Long pause = new paragraph

In hands-free mode, a pause of 2–3 seconds starts a new paragraph.

Instant start

Keeps the microphone ready so the first word is never clipped, with 0.4 s of pre-roll. Windows shows the mic-in-use icon all the time; nothing is recorded until you press the key.

Language & reliability

Spoken language

Auto-detect works well for English and Hinglish. Forcing Hindi usually returns Devanagari script.

Help Whisper punctuate

English only. Primes the recogniser with a punctuated sample so commas and full stops come out better.

Backup engine

If the main engine fails (rate limit, outage, bad key) it quietly retries on this one.

Offline mode (whisper.cpp)

Runs Whisper on your own computer: no internet, no limits, nothing leaves your PC. Pick “Offline” above once these are set.

1 · Download the program

Get a whisper.cpp release for your OS from github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp and unzip it.

2 · Download a model

For example ggml-base.bin, or ggml-small.bin for better accuracy, from huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp.

CPU threads

Offline AI polish still needs a Groq or OpenAI key; turn polish off for a fully offline setup.

AI polish

Turns what you said into what you meant: fillers gone, punctuation fixed, lists and numbers formatted.

Polish

Clean up my speech

Removes “um/uh/matlab”, fixes punctuation, keeps your meaning. Uses your Groq or OpenAI key and adds about half a second.

Default tone

Per-app rules below can override this.

Polish level

Light only removes fillers; Polished also tightens wording.

Smart formatting

Spoken lists become bullet, numbered or checklist lines (checkboxes in Notion and Obsidian), with paragraphs and tidy numbers. Try “first … second … third …”.

Hinglish in Roman letters

Never output Devanagari; Hindi words stay as “kaise ho”.
