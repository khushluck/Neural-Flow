General

Free, no card: console.groq.com/keys. Used for transcription and AI cleanup.

Hindi forced with hi usually comes out in Devanagari. Keep Auto and tick "Hinglish in Roman letters" under AI cleanup for Roman Hinglish.

Hotkey

Hold to talk. Esc cancels a recording. Right Ctrl is the safest on Windows (a lone Alt tap can focus an app's menu bar).

AI cleanup

Per-app tone

One rule per line: app=tone (matches the program name, e.g. slack=casual) or title:gmail=formal (matches the window title). Tones: neutral, casual, formal, code.

Dictionary & snippets

Personal dictionary

One entry per line. A plain word teaches the recogniser the spelling. heard => Right force-fixes a recurring mistake.

Snippets

Say the trigger on its own (optionally “insert …”) and the full text is typed. \n = new line.

History

Your last dictations, saved only on this computer. Click Copy if anything failed to paste.

Offline mode (whisper.cpp)

Runs Whisper on your own computer: no internet, no limits, nothing leaves your PC. Choose “Offline (whisper.cpp)” as the provider on the General tab after setting these up.

  1. Download a whisper.cpp release for your OS from github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/releases and unzip it.
  2. Download a model such as ggml-base.bin (or ggml-small.bin for better accuracy) from huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp.
  3. Point to both files below.

Offline AI cleanup still needs a Groq/OpenAI key; turn cleanup off for a fully offline setup.
